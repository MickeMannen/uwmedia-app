# Privacy policy

UWMedia works on the photos, videos and dive logs on your own device.
They stay there: the app has no account, no analytics, no advertising and
no crash reporting, and its author receives nothing from it.

This program will not transfer any information to other networked systems
unless specifically requested by the user or the person installing or
operating it.

## What the app sends, and when

- **The update check.** When you press "Check for updates" on the About
  page, the app asks GitHub (`api.github.com`) for the latest release of
  this project. GitHub sees the request as it sees any visit (your IP
  address); nothing about you, your media or your dive logs is in it. The
  app never checks by itself.
- **Links.** A link in the app (the project page, a dive computer maker's
  site) opens in your web browser, and what happens there is between you
  and that site.

Nothing else leaves the device. The tools packaged with the desktop app
(ffmpeg, ffprobe and exiftool) are run on your files locally.

## What the app keeps on the device

Your settings, the dive logs you import and the overlay templates you make
are saved in the app's own folders on the device. Removing the app's data
removes them.
